# 2. Problem Statement

## Current Problem

IT support teams commonly receive repeated questions about account access, VPN connectivity, software installation, and other routine issues. When answers are scattered across documents or depend on an agent's availability:

- Employees may wait longer for help.
- Support staff may repeatedly answer the same questions.
- Requests may be inconsistently documented or routed.

## Why AI Is Appropriate

- A **language model** can interpret natural-language questions.
- **RAG** can supply relevant, approved support documentation as context.
- **Function calling** can translate specific user intents into requests for predefined backend actions.

The application retains server-side validation, permission checks, audit logging, and human escalation. AI-generated text alone will **not** authorize or execute privileged operations.
